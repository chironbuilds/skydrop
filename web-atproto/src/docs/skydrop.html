Skydrop — send Tari to a Bluesky account

Skydrop is the first contract built on the layer. You send tTARI to someone's did:plc before they have an Ootle wallet. They collect by posting a one-line claim code and proving, in the contract, that they control the account. No one in the middle can take the money.

deposit

Escrow to a DID

Funds are escrowed under the recipient's permanent identifier, governed by ClaimPolicy, with a challenge window the sender chooses.

claim

Prove and collect

The recipient posts tari-claim:…, then submits the identity chain, signed commit and MST path. The contract verifies and records the claim.

challenge

Rotations win

A PLC log can't be proven complete. During the window, anyone can submit a newer chain from a rotated-away key to cancel a stale claim.

The app is at skydrop.tari.mw, but its contracts date from before the network's 0.49 reset, and moving it across is still to do. In the new design it is exactly one policy on the shared escrow. Tips, wallet linking, bounties, metered service payments and social recovery are each another policy — added without redeploying the vault or moving a single escrowed deposit.
